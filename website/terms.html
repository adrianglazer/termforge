Trial & lifetime purchase

The planned offer is a free download, an explicitly started seven-day full-feature trial, and a €14.99 one-time lifetime unlock. Other storefronts use Apple’s localized prices; the Apple purchase sheet shows the price payable. There is no subscription or automatic charge after the trial.

The trial begins with Apple’s verified acquisition of the trial product and lasts 168 hours. Reinstalling or restoring purchases under the same Apple Account does not restart it. At expiry, remote functionality stops, while saved local data and recovery, export, deletion, support, and purchase controls remain available under the app’s authentication requirements.

Lifetime access is a non-expiring app unlock while the purchase remains valid. It does not guarantee perpetual OS compatibility, uninterrupted connections, or access to unrelated future products. Refunded or revoked purchases may lose access when Apple reports the change.

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Loading products, purchasing, and restoring may require connectivity. Availability and the final offer are confirmed in the App Store and the app’s purchase screen; this website does not take payments or start trials.
