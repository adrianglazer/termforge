Purpose & scope

Termforge provides an iPhone interface for SSH terminals, saved workspaces, and SFTP operations on user-selected remote hosts. Commands run on those hosts. The app does not supply a local Linux environment, a hosted server, or a Termforge account.

Systems & credentials
